Domains, mail and machines for businesses that don't have an IT department.

Small practice, deliberately small client list. We keep domains and certificates renewed, set up Google Workspace properly, move mailboxes without losing anything, and maintain the DNS records that decide whether your mail gets delivered or dropped.

What we do

  • Domains, hosting & SSLAnnual renewal, records, certificate install and expiry monitoring.
  • Workspace deploymentAccounts, groups, shared drives, retention.
  • Mailbox migrationFrom Zoho, Microsoft 365, or an old IMAP host.
  • Email authenticationSPF, DKIM and DMARC so your mail stops landing in spam.
  • Endpoint supportLaptops, phones, printers, the usual.

How we work

Renewals are billed annually, in advance, on a fixed notice date. Support is by email and by remote session — the remote tool is a small installer we send when there's something to look at, and it stays on the machine so the next call is one click instead of ten minutes of instructions.

About

We work with owner-run businesses of five to fifty people, mostly in professional services around Tulsa and the surrounding area. Most of what we do is unglamorous: fixing mail flow, tightening records, untangling the setup somebody's cousin did in 2019. If you already have a competent admin, you probably don't need us.
